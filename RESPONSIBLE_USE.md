# Responsible use

Character Studio places real people's likenesses into new images. That is powerful and personal. By using it, you agree to use it responsibly.

## Consent first

- Only use photos of **yourself**, or of people who have **clearly agreed** to have their likeness used this way.
- Do not use photos of celebrities, public figures, strangers or anyone who has not consented.
- Consent can be withdrawn. If someone asks, delete their character and any results.

## Children

- Use children's photos only if you are their parent or legal guardian, or have that person's explicit permission.
- Keep every image of a child age-appropriate. Never create sexualised, exploitative or harmful content involving minors.

## No deception or harm

Do not use Character Studio to:

- impersonate someone or make it look like they did or said something they did not;
- create non-consensual intimate or sexual imagery of anyone;
- harass, bully, defame or threaten anyone;
- create content intended to mislead, such as fake evidence, fraud or political disinformation.

If you share a generated image, make clear that it is AI-generated wherever that could matter.

## Provider rules still apply

When you use the optional AI features, your images and prompts are processed by the provider you choose (Anthropic through Claude Code or the Anthropic API, OpenAI through Codex or the OpenAI API, Google through the Gemini API, or Black Forest Labs through FLUX) under **your own account and their terms and usage policies**. Character Studio does not bypass provider safety systems, and you must not try to. Some providers mark their images (for example, Google adds an invisible SynthID watermark).

## How the app helps

- Everything stays on your computer by default. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click an AI action.
- Every generation needs your explicit approval of the exact prompt, photos and mappings.
- **Replace a person** (region editing) only uses the reference photos you approved for that person; replacing someone with a different person needs a new approval.
- Edits never overwrite the original: each one is a new result that records what was changed.
- The app never identifies unknown people. You name your characters yourself.
- You can delete characters, photos and results at any time.
